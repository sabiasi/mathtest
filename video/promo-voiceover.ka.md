# Promo video: Georgian voice-over script

For the owner to read aloud over `rapido-promo.mp4` (4:07). One block per scene. The times come from
`src/promo/timeline.ts` (each scene starts with its 2.5 s title card). Each text is a little shorter
than its scene, so you can read calmly and leave pauses. Talk to the teacher as „თქვენ“.

Recording: one file per scene, named as below (e.g. `04-math.m4a`), in a quiet room, phone 20–30 cm
from your mouth. If one sentence goes wrong, record only that scene again.

---

## Reading tips

- **Speed**: calm, a bit slower than normal talking. A dash (—) = a short pause; a new line = a breath.
- **Button names** in „…“ (რეგისტრაცია, ფორმულა, ქაღალდზე ნახვა…) are the same words as on screen:
  say them a little more clearly.
- **Read „Rapido“ as „რაპიდო“.** Say it the same way every time.
- Smile slightly while reading: listeners can hear it. Talk the way you would to a colleague in the
  teachers' room, not like an advert.
- Before you record, read each scene once while the video plays to check it fits.
